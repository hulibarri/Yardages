Altitude

Enter the elevation where you hit your normal yardages (your home course). When Altitude is on, every number adjusts for the elevation where you're standing. 2% per 1,000 ft is the usual rule of thumb. Change it if you find your ball goes farther or shorter than that.

Backup

Everything saves on this phone automatically. A backup lets you move your yardages to a new phone or browser.
